ShiftBridge is a spatial workflow prototype designed to preserve operational context between work shifts.

In many shift-based workplaces, important information depends on informal messages, handwritten notes, or a short conversation between outgoing and incoming employees. When schedules do not overlap, recent incidents, pending activities, and priorities can easily be missed.

ShiftBridge provides a shared spatial handoff where employees can record Pending activities and time-sensitive Updates. Each activity includes its department, author, creation time, description, and—when useful—an optional photo attachment.

Incoming employees can quickly identify overdue work, review high- and low-priority activities, inspect recent updates, complete pending items, and consult an Audit log that preserves the sequence of actions across shifts.

Rather than functioning as a general-purpose task manager, ShiftBridge acts as a continuity layer between people performing the same operation at different times.
